Radial Menu Builder++ for Plasticity
(formerly Plasticity Radial Menu Builder)

This app changes files in your Plasticity folders. That's its job: it saves radial
menus, writes themes and keyboard shortcuts, and adds, renames, moves and
deletes matcaps and environments.

By using it you agree to that, and you use it at your own risk. It's provided as is,
and its creator isn't responsible for any loss or damage that comes from using it.

Most of this is easy to undo. Plasticity can reset its theme to defaults, disabled
matcaps and environments can be enabled again, and deleted ones go to the Recycle Bin
(the Trash on a Mac) first. A backup of your .plasticity folder never hurts.

It's up to you to read the Plasticity documentation and understand how the pieces fit
together. This app is a tool to make them fit more easily.

This app isn't made by or affiliated with the makers of Plasticity. The open-source
libraries it is built with are listed, with their licences, in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt.
